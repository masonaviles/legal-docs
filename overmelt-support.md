# Overmelt support

Questions, problems or ideas: email **mason.aviles.dev@gmail.com**.

It helps to include your phone model, the iOS or Android version, and what you were doing when
the problem happened.

## Common questions

**I bought Pro on another phone.** Open Settings → Restore a purchase while signed in to the same
Apple or Google account you bought it with.

**How do I get a refund?** Purchases go through Apple or Google, so refunds do too. On iPhone, go to
[reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com). On Android, use the Google Play order
history.

**Where is my data?** On your phone. Overmelt has no account and does not receive your collection,
photos or notes. The [privacy policy](overmelt-privacy.md) lists the few things the app sends.

**The spot price looks wrong or old.** Prices are indicative and may be delayed. Tap the price at
the top of Appraise, then the refresh button, or enter your own in Settings → Spot prices.
