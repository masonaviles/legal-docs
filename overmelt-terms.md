## What Overmelt is, and what it is not

Overmelt calculates the value of the metal in a piece of jewelry or bullion from the weight and
purity you enter and a metal price, and compares the price you enter with that value.

- **Its figures are estimates.** Melt values, multiples, markups, grades and payout ranges are
  calculated from what you enter. If a weight or purity is wrong, the result is wrong.
- **It does not test or authenticate anything.** It cannot tell whether a piece is real gold,
  what it is made of, or whether a stamp is genuine. It is not a substitute for a professional
  appraisal, an assay or a jeweler's inspection.
- **It is not financial, investment, tax or legal advice.** It does not tell you what to buy,
  sell or hold, and nothing in it is a recommendation or a forecast of prices.
- **Metal prices are indicative and may be delayed.** They come from a third-party service, are
  provided without warranty, and may differ from the price any dealer will use.
- **Benchmark and payout ranges are observations, not offers.** They describe prices seen at a
  stated date. No seller or buyer is bound by them, and what you are offered may be different.
- **Tax, shipping and fee figures are the ones you enter.** The app applies no tax table and says
  nothing about what is owed where you live.
- **Price alerts depend on your phone.** The operating system decides when the app may check
  prices in the background, so an alert can arrive late or not at all. Overmelt is not a market
  data or alerting service.
- **Documents the app creates are records, not valuations.** An insurance schedule or piece
  record lists what you entered and the metal value on the day it was made; it is not an
  appraisal and should not be presented as one.
